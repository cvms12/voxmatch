Legal Security & Architecture

1. Zero-Server Audio (DTLS-SRTP): All voice streams travel directly peer-to-peer via WebRTC encrypted with DTLS-SRTP. No audio is ever stored, logged, or routed through a recording server.

4. IP Privacy Notice: Standard WebRTC peer-to-peer connections exchange ICE network candidates (which may include IP addresses) to establish direct audio. For maximum anonymity, using a trusted VPN is recommended.
